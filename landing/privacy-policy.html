Advertiser inquiry form

Exit feedback

If you sign out or delete your account, we may ask an optional one-question survey (satisfaction score + comment). Skippable.

3. Legal basis / consent

  • Consent — for the waitlist and advertiser-inquiry forms, and for optional profile fields, we act on your explicit, opt-in consent captured at submission (an unticked checkbox you must actively check).
  • Performance of a contract — account data, impression/dwell data, and withdrawal data are processed because they are how the developer-pays-you or advertiser-runs-ads service actually works.
  • Legitimate interest — IP address and dwell-pattern data are used narrowly for fraud/abuse detection, which protects the integrity of the payout system for everyone.

You can withdraw consent for optional data (profile survey, marketing follow-up) at any time by emailing us; this does not affect data we must keep for the core service to function or for legal/accounting reasons (see Retention below).

Resend, GitHub, and Razorpay are third-party processors whose infrastructure may process or store data outside India. We have not audited each processor's current data-localization or standard-contractual-clause status as part of this review — if that matters for your situation, ask us or check each provider's own privacy/DPA documentation directly.

6. Retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the extension or dashboard. This immediately:

  • Anonymizes your email, UPI ID, company, and country on your account record;
  • Revokes all active sign-in sessions.

Impression, click, and withdrawal rows are kept (linked to an anonymous internal ID, not your email) because they are financial/accounting records — advertiser billing and your own payout history depend on them. Advertiser inquiry data is kept for as long as reasonably needed for the business relationship and accounting, then deleted or anonymized on request where we are not legally required to keep it.

8. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

DevCut is operated from India and most users are expected to be India-based, so the DPDP Act, 2023 is relevant. A few important caveats, stated honestly rather than glossed over:

  • The DPDP Act's substantive obligations come into force in stages, via notified Rules and commencement dates set by the government (the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 lay out phased timelines for different obligations). We are not asserting that every provision of the Act is currently in force — check the Ministry of Electronics and IT's official notifications for the current commencement status of any specific obligation before relying on this section for a legal position.
  • Regardless of exact commencement dates, we've written this policy to already reflect DPDP-style principles — purpose limitation, consent, a named grievance contact, and a deletion mechanism — because that's good practice independent of enforcement timing.

9. Security

We use hashed (not raw) session tokens, environment-variable-based secrets (never hardcoded in source), parameterized database queries, and rate limiting on public endpoints. No system is unbreakable, and we make no claim that DevCut is "100% secure" — if you find a vulnerability, please report it to techsupport@devcut.co.in before disclosing it publicly.

10. Children

DevCut is a professional developer tool, not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and we don't run age verification — accordingly, we don't run ad categories (gambling, adult content, speculative crypto) that would depend on one. See our advertiser policy.
